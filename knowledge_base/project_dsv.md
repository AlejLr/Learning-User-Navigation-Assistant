# DSV SDG Challenge — Wave-Based EV Charging Optimization

## Overview

A sustainability-focused operations research project developed in partnership with DSV, a global logistics company. The goal was to optimize the charging workflow for DSV's electric truck fleet to minimize energy costs while ensuring operational readiness.

## Role

**Team Captain.** Led a multidisciplinary team through problem definition, solution design, implementation, and pilot deployment.

## Problem

Electric truck fleets face a cost challenge: charging during peak electricity pricing hours inflates energy costs significantly. Uncoordinated charging also risks grid overload and reduces battery longevity.

## Solution

Designed and implemented a **wave-based charging workflow**: trucks are grouped into charging waves scheduled during off-peak electricity pricing windows. The algorithm accounts for departure schedules, battery state-of-charge, and grid capacity constraints.

## Results

- Piloted on **10 trucks** in a real DSV logistics operation
- Achieved approximately **80% reduction in charging costs** compared to the unoptimized baseline
- Workflow validated for operational feasibility

## Tech Stack

- Python (optimization logic, scheduling algorithm)
- pandas and NumPy for data analysis
- Visualization of charging schedules and cost comparisons

## Impact

This project directly addressed SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) and SDG 13 (Climate Action) by reducing the operational cost barrier to EV adoption in logistics.
